Shekoko Child Safety Standards

Shekoko is a chat app for adults aged 18 and over only. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, and any sexual content involving minors.

What is forbidden

  • Using Shekoko if you are under 18.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to any sexual content involving minors.
  • Contacting, grooming or trying to meet a minor, or helping anyone to do so.

Breaking these rules leads to immediate and permanent account removal, and reporting to the authorities.

How we keep minors out and protect users

  • Accounts must state an age of 18 or over; accounts stating a lower age cannot be used.
  • Messages are automatically checked for signs that a user may be under 18; matches go to a human reviewer.
  • Photos from people you don't know yet are blurred until you choose to view them.
  • Groups are small, and only people who already chat with each other can be added.
  • Every user can block and report anyone from their profile or a chat. Reports keep the conversation as evidence for review.

How to report

  • In the app: open the person's profile and tap Report (you can also block them).
  • By e-mail: shekokochat@gmail.com. Include the Shekoko ID of the account if you can.

We review child-safety reports with priority, remove offending accounts, preserve evidence and report child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities (such as the police and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, NCMEC) as required by law.

Contact for child safety

Our child-safety contact is the Shekoko safety team, reachable at shekokochat@gmail.com.
