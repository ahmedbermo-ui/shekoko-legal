Delete your Shekoko account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Shekoko and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

You can also choose Delete Account & Exit in the exit window (the exit button at the top of the app).

Without the app

If you can't open the app any more, send an e-mail to shekokochat@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your 6-digit Shekoko ID. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

Delete some of your data, keep your account

  • A message, photo or voice message: long-press it → Delete (also for the other person).
  • A whole conversation: open the person's profile → Clear messages or Delete conversation.
  • Notes and reminders: delete them on the person's profile, or in Settings → Messages → Reminders.
  • Profile details: change or remove your nickname and other details in Settings.
  • Anything else: e-mail shekokochat@gmail.com with your 6-digit Shekoko ID and what you want deleted. We reply within 30 days.

Good to know: deleted text messages disappear from the app right away, but for safety a copy is kept in a locked archive for 1 year (for example to investigate abuse or answer lawful requests from the police), then permanently erased. Only the Shekoko safety team can open it. Photos and voice messages are not kept.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted right away: your profile, your login, your chats, favourites, notes, reminders and settings. Photos and voice messages are deleted.
  • Kept for a limited time in a locked safety archive that only the Shekoko safety team can open: a copy of your profile, text messages, calls and coin records for 1 year, then permanently erased. Purchase records are kept as long as accounting law requires (in Norway up to 5 years). This is to investigate abuse and answer lawful requests from the police.
  • Unused coins are lost when the account is deleted.
