Shekoko Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Shekoko ("we", "us") is a chat app for adults (18+) to meet and talk with new people. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with and what you can do about it.

The app is provided by Ahmed Bermo, Norway. Contact: shekokochat@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the information described here.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Account: your 6-digit Shekoko ID, nickname (optional), gender, age you declared, country, app language, chosen avatar. We do not ask for your real name, e-mail address or phone number. To create your account, show your profile card to others, and keep the service for adults only.
Messages and content: text messages, photos, voice messages, reactions, polls, scheduled messages, auto-reply text, group names and notes. To deliver your messages to the people you chat with.
Calls: who called whom, when, how long, and the coins charged. Shekoko does not record or store the sound or video of calls. To connect calls and charge the per-minute price shown before the call.
Activity: whether you are online and when you were last active. To show who is available in the Room. You can hide your last-seen time in Settings.
Coins and purchases: coin balance, coin history, and Google Play order details (order number, product, purchase token). We never see your card or bank details. To give you the coins you paid for and prevent fraud.
Safety: reports you send or receive, people you block, account warnings or suspensions. To keep Shekoko safe and enforce our Terms.
Settings: privacy switches (hide age, coins, last seen, read receipts), notification settings, muted chats, favourites and the names you give to contacts. To make the app work the way you chose.
Your own notes and reminders: private notes you write about a person, and reminders you set ("remind me to reply / to contact"). Only you can see them. To show your notes and send your reminders at the time you chose.
Time zone: your phone's time-zone offset (for example "UTC+2"). Not your location. To show the people you chat with what time it is for you ("2:14 AM for them").
Games and shared contacts: moves in mini games you play in a chat, and contacts you share (the shared person's Shekoko ID). To run the games and show shared contacts in the chat.
Translations: messages you choose to translate, and the translation. To translate them (see section 4) and show the translation again without charging you twice.
Device: a push-notification token for your phone. To notify you about new messages and calls.
Advertising: Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage. To show ads that pay for the free service. See section 4.
Support: messages you send to Shekoko Support. To answer your questions and complaints.

Done on your phone only: the language of a message is detected on your phone (to decide whether to offer translation), and your photo-blur, text-size, font and translation preferences are stored only on your phone.

Automated safety checks: to protect minors, messages are automatically checked for signs that someone may be under 18. A match only creates a report for a human reviewer; nobody is suspended automatically by this check.

Your app lock PIN is stored only on your phone, never on our servers. Your password and your recovery code are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we can't read them.

2. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

We use your information to perform our contract with you (providing the service), for our legitimate interests (safety, preventing abuse and fraud, improving the app), to meet legal obligations (for example accounting rules), and with your consent where required (for example personalised ads). You can withdraw consent at any time.

3. Who can see what

  • Other users can see your profile card (ID, nickname, avatar, gender, country, and your age and coins unless you hide them) and messages you send them.
  • In groups, all members see the messages, the member list and the pinned note. Polls are anonymous: no one, including other members, can see how you voted, only the totals.
  • Shekoko staff may review content that has been reported, to handle reports and keep users safe.

5. How long we keep it

  • Photos and voice messages: deleted automatically after 30 days, or right away when the message is deleted. "Play once" voice messages and "view once" photos are deleted after being opened. They are not kept in the safety archive.
  • Messages: until you or the other person delete them, or your account is deleted. Deleted text messages disappear from the app immediately, but are kept in a locked safety archive for 1 year (for example to investigate abuse or answer lawful requests from the police) and then permanently erased. Only the Shekoko safety team can access this archive.
  • Reports: when you report someone, the conversation between you and that person is saved as evidence for up to 1 year, then erased.
  • Calls and coin movements: kept for 1 year, also after your account is deleted.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as accounting law requires (in Norway up to 5 years), also after your account is deleted.
  • Notes, reminders and translations: deleted with your account. Sent reminders are removed after 30 days.
  • Reports and safety records: as long as needed to keep the service safe.
  • When you delete your account: your account disappears from Shekoko immediately. A copy of your profile, text messages, calls and coin records is kept in the locked archive for 1 year, then permanently erased.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Change your profile and privacy switches in Settings at any time.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account (see also how to delete your account).
  • Block or report anyone from their profile.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or limit its use, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet). Contact us at shekokochat@gmail.com with your Shekoko ID.

7. Adults only

Shekoko is only for people aged 18 or older. Accounts that state an age under 18 cannot be used, and we close accounts when we learn a user is a minor. If you believe a minor is using Shekoko, please report them in the app or contact us.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Photos and voice messages can only be opened by the people in the chat, and only through short-lived links. No system is completely secure, so please don't share information you wouldn't want others to see.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are important, we'll tell you in the app before they apply.
